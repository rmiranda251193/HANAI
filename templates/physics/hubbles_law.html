Idealized model: the non-relativistic regime where recession speed stays well under the speed of light, so z = v / c is an accurate approximation of the real redshift. The Hubble constant itself is a genuine, currently-disputed measurement (the "Hubble tension") — not a fixed constant like the speed of light. Values are computed from v = H0 d and z = v / c — in the browser as you experiment, and again on the server when you submit. Never by AI.
